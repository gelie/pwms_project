{% comment %} The participants table on a delegation report: who is on the delegation, the form that adds someone, a per-row removal, and — below — the people who have been taken off it. Removing a participant is a soft delete: the row is kept with who removed them and when (see ``pwms.models.DelegationParticipant.remove``), and is listed here muted so the report still shows who was on the delegation. The count in the header is the delegation as it stands now. Rendered standalone as the HTMX response to an add or a remove, and included by ``pwms/delegation-report-detail.html``. The response root and the include target are both ``#participants-section``; callers swap it with ``outerHTML``. Context (from ``pwms.views._participants_context``): report, participants, removed_participants, can_edit_participants, participant_form; plus participants_status ({level, message}) when a participant was just added or removed. {% endcomment %}
