{% comment %} A meeting's attendance register: the hosting group's members (generated) plus any invitees added by hand (a PWMS user outside the hosting group, or a guest with no account), their RSVP and the attendance recorded during or after the sitting, with who recorded it and when. The header, the hosting-group note and the counts stay visible; the register table and the add-invitee form sit behind a collapse that is **closed by default**. Any panel action re-renders the fragment expanded (``attendance_expanded``), so saving a change never hides the table. Rendered standalone as the HTMX response to an add/record/remove/regenerate/ notify action and included by ``pwms/meeting-detail.html`` for the first paint. The response root and the include target are both ``#meeting-attendance``; callers swap it with ``outerHTML``. Context (see ``pwms.views._meeting_attendance_context``): meeting, attendance_rows, attendance_counts, can_edit_attendance, attendance_form, attendance_expanded, attendance_status (optional: {level, message}). {% endcomment %}
