{% comment %} The resolutions adopted at the engagement on a delegation report: the ones the report captured, and the form that captures another (BR02.3.9). Each resolution is an InternationalResolution of its own, nested under the report through ``WorkflowRelationship`` rather than owned by it (see ``pwms.views._create_child_resolution``), so the table links out to the resolution and reads the fields that instance carries. This card is the report's one listing of its resolutions: the Related tab drops the Hierarchy card entirely for a report (see the ``hierarchy`` override in ``pwms/delegation-report-detail.html``), since a report can never have a parent. Rendered standalone as the HTMX response to an add, and included by ``pwms/delegation-report-detail.html``. The response root and the include target are both ``#resolutions-section``; callers swap it with ``outerHTML``, and the status alert lives outside it in the tab pane so it survives that swap. Context (from ``pwms.views._resolutions_context``): report, resolutions, can_add_resolutions, resolution_form; plus resolutions_status ({level, message}) when one was just captured. {% endcomment %}
