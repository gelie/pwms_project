What it is

PWMS keeps a single record of each parliamentary instrument — delegation reports, international resolutions, international agreements and bills — as it moves through the stages of its passage. Every instrument has a current stage, a named owner and a deadline, and each step it takes is recorded against the person who took it.

What you see once signed in

  • Where each instrument has reached, who owns it and when it is due.
  • Referrals to committees, with the response deadline on each.
  • Who may view, edit or progress an instrument — and in which stage.
  • The full history of every change and every stage transition.
