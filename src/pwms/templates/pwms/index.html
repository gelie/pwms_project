Welcome to PWMS

This is the home page of the Parliament Workflow Management System.

About the System

The Parliament Workflow Management System (PWMS) is designed to support the day-to-day operations of parliamentary business. It helps staff and members track the lifecycle of motions, bills, and other parliamentary documents as they move through each stage of the legislative process.

Key Features

  • Create, review, and track motions and bills from drafting to resolution.
  • Manage committee assignments, agendas, and meeting outcomes.
  • Monitor workflow stages and approvals with a clear audit trail.
  • Search and access historical records of parliamentary business.
  • Role-based access to keep sensitive information secure.

Getting Started

Use the navigation menu to browse motions, committees, and sittings. New users should review their assigned role to confirm which actions they are permitted to perform. For assistance, contact the parliamentary IT support team.
