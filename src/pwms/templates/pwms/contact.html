Contact

Who to speak to about access, roles and problems with PWMS.

Access, groups and roles

PWMS access follows group membership. An administrator records you as a member of a group with a role, and your capabilities follow from that. If you cannot see an instrument you expect to, or cannot perform a step, speak to the administrator who maintains your committee's or unit's memberships — it is usually a question of whether that membership is current.

Problems with the system

For errors, unexpected behaviour, or anything that looks wrong in an instrument's history, contact the parliamentary IT support team. Include the instrument's reference number and what you were trying to do, so the problem can be traced in the audit trail.

Before you report a problem

  • Note the instrument's reference number — for example DR-2026-0001 for a delegation report, or a bill's number.
  • Say which action you were attempting: viewing, editing, transitioning or deleting.
  • If a button or menu item is missing, it is usually a permission rather than a fault — PWMS hides the actions you are not permitted to perform. See About for how access works.
