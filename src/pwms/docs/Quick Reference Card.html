PWMS — IRPD Quick Reference

Parliamentary Workflow Management System · Delegation Reports · International Resolutions · International Agreements

Sign in & your day

  • Sign in with your Parliament directory account — no separate PWMS password. Use the show/hide control to check your typing.
  • Dashboard figures: Total workflows (open · unassigned) · Assigned to you (you owe the next action) · Due in 7 days · Overdue.
  • Work lists: Assigned to you · Due soon · Overdue · Awaiting your committees.
  • Overdue = deadline passed while the record is still open. A record in a final stage is never overdue.
  • The bell shows alerts. The search box in the page header is decorative — use the Search on the page you are working in.

The three lifecycles

Delegation Report
Awaiting PGIR approval → Submitted for tabling → Tabled and referred to Committee → Closed – House approved
DR-YYYY-NNNN (issued by PWMS)

International Resolution
Captured → Assigned → In Progress → Implemented → Closed
number is typed by you and must be unique

International Agreement
(Submitted for tabling) → Agreement Tabled – referred to Committee [start] → Committee considering and processing → Committee submitted report for tabling → House adopted – referred to Department → Closed – House approved
IA-YYYY-NNNN (issued by PWMS)

A new agreement starts at stage 2, not at Submitted for tabling. Reference numbers restart at 0001 each January — always quote the year.

Create a record

  • Common to all three: Title (required), Description, Assigned to, Deadline, Priority.
  • Delegation Report (New Report): engagement name, country → city, start/end dates (never future; end ≥ start), report document link, delegates (member or support official + role held), resolutions adopted.
  • International Resolution (New Resolution): resolution number (required, unique), delegation report (optional), adoption date, responsible group, resolution text, implementation progress.
  • International Agreement (New Agreement): agreement type 231(2)/231(3), ATC tabling date, submitting department, responsible minister, referral committees, ATC reference, agreement + memorandum links.
Minister: link a serving office holder in Responsible minister. For a former minister or one with no account, type the name in Responsible minister name — PWMS refuses anyone holding no executive appointment.

Move a record on

  • Toolbar: Document (PDF/HTML) · Status · Edit · Delete — each appears only if you may use it.
  • Status lists only the moves legal from the current stage. Each opens Change status: add a Comment (good practice — it lands on the Timeline), then Confirm status change. You land on the Timeline.
  • Taking a move validates it, advances the stage, writes the history entry, and alerts the people who need to know.
  • No Status menu = you may read or edit the record but not move it on (the usual case for a committee it was referred to).
Blocked? A warning — This transition cannot be taken yet — lists every unmet requirement and disables the button. Closing a delegation report needs an ATC update published entry, or PWMS answers “Requires a 'ATC update published' event on this workflow.”

Referrals

  • Raise: Referrals tab → Add Referral → Refer to, Response due (always set it), notes → Send referral. Needs Edit.
  • Grants the committee view, plus edit while the referral is open, and keeps view after it closes. It grants no right to move the record on.
  • Answer: Respond → response document link + notes → Record response. The linked committee may answer; otherwise an editor, on its behalf.
  • Withdraw: the person who raised it. Reminders go out at 24 h and 1 h; unanswered referrals expire.

Documents (SharePoint)

  • Attach: Attachments tab → Add attachment → browse Sites you are a member of → Attach an existing file, or choose a file + Attachment type → Upload & attach. Needs Edit.
  • Open: select the document name. Never copy or email the resolved address — it expires in about an hour. Share the record instead.
  • Versions shows the history with a Download per version. Same name into the same folder = a new version, not a second document.
  • Detach removes the link only; the file stays in SharePoint. Activity is recorded on the record.

Notes — two kinds

  • The record's Notes field — free text captured on the create/edit form and shown as Notes on the record.
  • The note log — dated entries on the Notes tab (Add Note; needs Edit).
  • A note may be changed or deleted only by its author, and only while they may still edit the record. Add a correcting note instead.

Reports & exports

  • Reports → Report builder: filter by report type, search, priority, sort, workflow type, state, owner, group, period or explicit dates, overdue / due soon / unassigned.
  • A report — and its export, print or share — shows only records you may open. Never more.
  • Exports: Excel (.xlsx) · PDF (.pdf) · Web page (.html) · Comma-separated (.csv). CSV is the register only; the others always carry summary, breakdowns, register, activity and referrals, whichever report type you picked.
  • One instrument as a formal document: the record's Document menu.
Share: link + expiry, optional repeat and email. The recipient must sign in and sees your figures. It is fixed once created, and only an administrator can revoke it. A share dies if its creator's account is removed.

Alerts

  • Bell = unread count and the 5 newest. View all alerts lists the newest 25; Mark all read clears every unread, not just those 25.
  • Opening an alert marks it read; viewing the page marks nothing.
  • Raised on: record created · record moved on · referral raised, answered, recalled, expired or due.
  • The person who acted is never alerted, and nobody who cannot view the record is ever told about it.

Escalate when…

  • New Report / Resolution / Agreement refuses you — ask your administrator for the creating role.
  • A record is missing from your register — ask for access, or for a referral to your committee.
  • The Status menu is missing or the move you expect is absent — confirm your move-on right, then check the Diagram tab.
  • A transition is blocked — read the warning box; a report close normally needs the ATC update.
  • Attachments show no SharePoint sites — ask for the site to be enabled and your membership synced.
  • No alert email arrives — ask the administrator to check the mail service and the background worker.
  • A share must be revoked — only an administrator can; do not rely on the link meanwhile.
